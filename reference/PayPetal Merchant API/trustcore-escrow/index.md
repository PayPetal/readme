---
title: TrustCore Escrow
excerpt: >-
  TrustCore is PayPetal's merchant-controlled escrow product. Merchants initiate
  an agreement on behalf of a customer, generating a secure payment link. The
  customer funds the escrow through that link, and the merchant holds full
  control over when funds are released — enabling safe, trust-based transactions
  between buyers and sellers.
